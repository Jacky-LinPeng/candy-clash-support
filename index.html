Play a little. Play together.

A sweet rivalry.
A room full of friends.

Match candies, create special pieces, and challenge your friends on the same Wi-Fi. Here’s how to get everyone into the game.

One device

Practice at your pace

Choose Solo Practice and a difficulty. Swap adjacent candies, create special pieces, and attack your opponents. No account or internet connection required.

Two to four players

Bring everyone together

Install the same app version on each device. Connect to the same Wi-Fi, open Play with Friends, and allow Local Network access.

Your first room

  1. One player creates a room. Friends choose that room from the list.
  2. At least two human players join and mark themselves ready.
  3. The host starts the match. Each player acts on their own turn.
  4. When the match ends, get ready for another round.

AI can fill spare playing seats. A friends match still needs at least two human players to start.

A little help

Why can’t I see my friend’s room?

Check that every device uses the same Wi-Fi and app version, and that Local Network access is enabled for Candy Clash in iOS Settings. Keep the host app open. Some public, guest, school, or office networks prevent devices from communicating; use a network that allows local device connections, then try again.

Can I watch a full room?

Yes, when spectator space is available. Rooms support up to four playing seats and four spectators. Joining a match already in progress also uses spectator mode. Request a seat for the next round; the host can assign an available seat between matches.

What happens if someone disconnects?

An AI temporarily takes over a disconnected player’s active seat. Returning to the same room during the retained session can restore that seat at a turn boundary. Closing the app may lose the temporary recovery credential.

Why did the whole room end?

The host device coordinates the game. If the host leaves, locks the device, or loses the connection, the room may end. Keep the host app in the foreground. There is no automatic host transfer.

Do we need an internet connection?

No internet access is required for a match. Local multiplayer needs a shared local network that lets the devices communicate. The app does not connect players across the internet.

How do I change sound or motion?

Open Settings to adjust sound, music, haptics, and reduced motion. Your preferences stay on your device.
