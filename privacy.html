Privacy policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Candy Clash Together is developed by Peng Lin. For privacy questions, contact 540933120@qq.com.

On your device

The app stores game preferences, your local nickname and avatar choice, and your best solo result on your device. These records restore your preferences and show your result. The app does not require an account.

Local multiplayer

When you choose to play with friends, the app uses your local network to discover rooms and exchange room details, nicknames, game actions, and match state. The host device coordinates the match. Other room members can see your nickname and game results. Choose a nickname that does not reveal personal information you do not wish to share.

The connection exposes network addressing information to participating devices as needed to communicate. Room discovery is visible on the local network.

Temporary session credentials

The host creates a temporary recovery credential so a disconnected player can return to the same session. It is kept in app memory for that session, is not advertised in room discovery, and is not included in public room snapshots. Closing the app may remove it.

Game services and analytics

The app uses no developer-operated account or game server, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK. Match data is exchanged between devices on the local network. We do not receive gameplay data through an in-app collection service.

Apple may separately process download, diagnostic, or platform data under its own policies and your device settings.

Permissions

Local Network access is used to find and connect to game rooms on the same network. You can change access in iOS Settings. Solo practice remains available without this permission. The app does not request contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone access.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include so we can respond. Avoid sending account passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information. The email provider processes mail according to its own service policies.

Removing data

You can remove local app data by deleting the app. System backups may retain or restore data depending on your settings. Contact 540933120@qq.com for privacy questions or to request removal of information you have sent directly to support.

Website hosting

These support pages are hosted using GitHub Pages. Visiting this website sends a request to the hosting service, including network information such as your IP address and browser information. The host may process request and log data under its privacy policy. We have not added advertising, analytics scripts, or contact forms to these pages.

The support website opens in your system browser and is separate from local gameplay. Opening a website or sending an email requires an internet connection.
